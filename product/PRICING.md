# Pricing direction

## Status

These numbers are **historical working anchors recovered from earlier MetaBot productization work**.

They are not a promise that every current engagement will be quoted at these exact prices.

Scope, integrations, governance, security, deployment requirements and business value matter more than raw agent count.

## Historical public anchors

| Offer | Historical starting price |
|---|---:|
| AI Workforce Assessment | **$2,500** |
| First Digital Workforce | **$8,000** |
| Department Workforce | **$15,000** |
| Multi-Department Workforce | **$35,000** |
| Enterprise Workforce | **$60,000+** |
| Enterprise Transformation | **$150,000+** |
| Workforce Operations | **$1,000–$15,000+/month** |

## Pricing principle

Do not primarily sell “agents.”

Sell the designed business capability.

Internal scoping can consider:

```text
discovery
+ digital roles
+ departments
+ integrations
+ workflow complexity
+ governance
+ deployment
+ support / operations
```

## Why

A customer is paying for much more than model access:

- process discovery;
- architecture;
- integrations;
- testing;
- governance;
- human checkpoints;
- deployment;
- evidence;
- reliability;
- ongoing operation.

## Enterprise

Enterprise Autonomous Company OS engagements should generally be custom scoped.

A complex deployment may include:

- executive layer;
- multiple departments;
- security architecture;
- auditability;
- private integrations;
- change management;
- managed operations;
- executive reporting.

## Commercial objective

The current priority is to turn the product ladder into:

- paid assessments;
- bounded pilots;
- recurring workforce operations;
- larger enterprise deployments;
- licensing / partnerships.

Pricing should evolve with real customer evidence.
