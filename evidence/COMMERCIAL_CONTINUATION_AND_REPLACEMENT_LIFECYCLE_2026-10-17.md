## 2. Cross-wake execution gap discovered

The successful effect exposed a structural lifecycle gap rather than hiding it.

The native verified-follow-up executor required the decision-recording session to equal the current execution session. Hermes scheduled wakes use new session identities, so a valid durable CEO decision that survives beyond its recording wake cannot automatically be actuated by a later wake through that executor.

The observed effect therefore used the governed sender's documented execution path, not a recovered cross-wake durable-intent execution.

Public conclusion:

> Durable decision provenance is proven; durable cross-wake consequential execution is not yet proven.

The intended direction is to preserve the original decision identity while re-evaluating current authority, veto/revocation, role, execution service, work scope, resource/destination, suppression, capacity and idempotency at execution time.
