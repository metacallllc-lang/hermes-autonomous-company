# Sponsorship and infrastructure support

## What we are looking for

MetaBot is seeking **non-dilutive support** that increases the quality, reliability and speed of the Autonomous Company OS.

High-value support includes:

- model / inference credits;
- cloud compute credits;
- API credits;
- GPU or workstation hardware;
- hardware evaluation units;
- hosted infrastructure;
- security testing;
- observability tooling;
- research collaboration;
- enterprise pilot sponsorship.

## Why support matters

The system can route between models, but stronger reasoning models and more reliable infrastructure increase the amount of difficult work that can be delegated economically.

Infrastructure support can therefore directly fund:

- better executive reasoning;
- larger-context analysis;
- commercial research;
- production testing;
- verification;
- model fallback and redundancy;
- productization;
- customer deployments.

## Sponsor categories

Possible relationships include:

### Infrastructure sponsor

Provide compute, model credits, API credits or hardware.

### Research sponsor

Support governance, safety, verification, human-AI collaboration or autonomous-company research.

### Enterprise pilot sponsor

Fund a real deployment and receive a bounded implementation with measurable business objectives.

### Founding corporate sponsor

Support the public evidence program and production-scale experimentation over a longer period.

## What sponsors do not receive automatically

Sponsorship does not automatically grant:

- control of the company;
- root authority;
- access to credentials;
- access to private customer data;
- access to private source code;
- permission to weaken governance.

Any additional rights require a separate explicit agreement.

## Use of support

Support may be allocated to:

- model inference;
- compute;
- integrations;
- security;
- testing;
- customer deployments;
- productization;
- independent verification;
- documentation and public evidence.

## Contact

For sponsorship or pilot discussions, contact MetaBot Solutions through its public company channels or open a GitHub issue that contains **no confidential information** and request a private follow-up.
