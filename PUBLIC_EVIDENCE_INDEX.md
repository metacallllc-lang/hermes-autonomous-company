## Evidence classes

### SUPPORTED

Direct or independently corroborated evidence exists for the scoped claim.

### SUPPORTED, SCOPED

Evidence supports a narrower statement than a broad marketing interpretation.

### NOT PROVEN

The evidence needed to make the claim confidently is absent, stale, contradictory or incomplete.

### HISTORICAL

The fact or claim was true / recorded at a prior time but is not automatically current.

## Public standard

When two records conflict, the current public repository should prefer:

1. authority;
2. direct evidence;
3. freshness;
4. independent verification;
5. explicit scoping.

A polished dashboard is not allowed to silently outrank stronger evidence.
