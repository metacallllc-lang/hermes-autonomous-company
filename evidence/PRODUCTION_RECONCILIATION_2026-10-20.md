This dated record summarizes the latest verified operating boundary for the separately operated MetaBot / MetaCall autonomous-company environment. It is a sanitized public reconciliation, not a source-code release and not proof of commercial success.

## What changed

October 20 closed several continuation defects without adding a second CEO, scheduler, queue, control plane or authoritative store:

- **anti-stale economic discovery:** before a pre-revenue company can settle on WAITING, independent economic lanes must be freshly observed or exactly fenced; source failure does not establish no demand;

- **inference-capacity truth:** temporary model-capacity exhaustion now has an exact `INFERENCE_CAPACITY_UNAVAILABLE` disposition and route cooldown semantics instead of generic failure;
- **active funding runway:** `funding_runway` is an independent due-driven discovery lane for grants, credits, accelerators, sponsorships and other resource opportunities;
- **post-executive reconciliation:** a retired child can no longer leave its linked opportunity indefinitely `DISPATCHED`; terminal parent disposition and event acknowledgement are reconciled through the existing lifecycle;
- **economic work outranks mission progress:** open mission criteria are mission-progress evidence, not a standing global prerequisite. Due buyer, follow-up, marketplace, MetaCall, MetaBot, funding, partnership and sponsorship work outrank mission-only review.
