## Classification

**Core v1: BUILT / PRODUCTION**

This means the core autonomous-company operating architecture is treated as a production baseline. It does **not** mean commercial validation is complete, every integration is healthy, or the business objective has been achieved.

Ordinary repairs, model changes, tool changes, new channels, workflow changes and product evolution are production operations — not a new build mission.

## Authority

- Human owner retains **root authority + veto**.
- Protected authority cannot be expanded by the CEO, C-suite, workers or optimization loops.
- One canonical Hermes-native CEO loop remains the company operating loop.
- C-suite / Bot Chat is an executive deliberation surface and must converge on canonical company state.
- A missing or stale authority record blocks only the affected action rather than silently expanding permission.
