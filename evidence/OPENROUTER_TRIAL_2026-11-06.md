# OpenRouter C-suite field trial — November 6, 2026

MetaBot is a division of MetaCall LP. VEXO is the public build identity for this
work on governed autonomous company operations using Nous Research's Hermes Agent.

## What changed

All six executive profiles had already been switched to OpenRouter by the owner.
The private integration still pinned company jobs and physical requests to Nous,
so configuration alone could not switch the autonomous execution path. A new
owner-authorized, exact-model trial connects CEO, CFO, COO, CRO, CMO and CTO routing
to `deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017` on OpenRouter.

Admission retains exact work and role bindings, finite job/request bounds, no
cross-model fallback, immutable failed attempts and conservative outstanding
exposure. Prior Nous policies and history remain intact. The owner manages
provider credit; the integration does not purchase credit automatically.
New paid trial admission closes at its recorded 48-hour deadline.

## Verified scope

- One native inert TOOL exercise and one separate CEO decision exercise passed:
  four physical requests, no business actions, provider-settled receipts.
- All six roles resolve the exact OpenRouter/model pair. Five served executive
  profiles and the default profile were independently observed ready on one
  Hermes gateway after a clean restart.
- Real interactive Hermes sessions then recorded the requested provider/model,
  model calls and tool use. Interactive use is not autonomous company output.
- Forty-one focused offline checks passed: paid-trial admission/transport (14),
  native job budgets (13), and asynchronous assignment lifecycle (14).
- Streaming settlement now processes a received terminal cost receipt when the
  SDK closes its stream before exhausting the HTTP iterator. Missing receipts
  retain their maximum exposure; costs are not guessed or cleared by timeout.

## Measurement window

The first ordinary paid physical admission was **2026-11-06 16:36:27 UTC**.
The deadline is **2026-11-08 16:36:27 UTC** — 10:36 AM on both dates in Guatemala.
Qualification requests are outside this window. An hourly local monitor records
observations and prepares the final report after the deadline. Local observation
depends on the machine and application remaining available.

The report distinguishes configured routes, actual native sessions, autonomous
versus interactive use, assignment reconciliation, model/tool calls, errors,
settled company costs and outstanding exposure. New opportunities, clients,
collected revenue and funding awards require independent business evidence.
Provider-wide usage may include other activity; resumed session counters can
include earlier turns. Neither is automatically attributed to the experiment.
This is an operational before/after observation, not a controlled causal model
benchmark. Downtime and differences in selected work will be disclosed.

## Acceptance and commercial limits

The latest complete company audit remains 133/149 passing (November 3).
Separate repaired reruns now produce 147/149 latest results, with scheduler-history
and role-provenance assertions still unresolved. A fresh full passing audit has
not been established. The separate private source archive remains under review.

Public Hermes fork PR #3 is merged; PR #4 is open and unmerged. That fork does
not ship private AI-OS integration source. The present model migration does not
establish successful execution by all six roles, a new client, collected revenue,
a funding award, self-funding or continuous commercial autonomy.
